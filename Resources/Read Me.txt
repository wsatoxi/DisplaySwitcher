Display Switcher
================

Press a shortcut to open a "Display Mode" panel at the top right of the screen,
then switch between display modes (Extend / <display> Only / Mirror).
It works much like Win+P on Windows.

Requires macOS 14 or later (Apple silicon / Intel).


■ Install

1. Drag "Display Switcher" onto "Applications" on the right.
2. Open Display Switcher from the Applications folder.

The first time you open it, macOS says it "cannot be opened" or that
"Apple could not verify" it. This is because the app is not notarized by Apple.
Open it like this:

  1. Click "Done" in the dialog
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security
  3. Near the bottom, next to "“Display Switcher” was blocked to protect your Mac",
     click "Open Anyway" and allow it with your password (or Touch ID)
  4. Open Display Switcher again and click "Open Anyway"

Once it is running, an icon with two displays appears in the menu bar.
It also starts automatically when you log in (you can turn this off in its menu).


■ How to use

  Press and release ⌃⌘P (Control + Command + P)
      → The Display Mode panel opens (and stays open)
  While the panel is open, press ⌃⌘P or ↑↓
      → Move the selection
  Return or click
      → Switch to that mode
  Hold ⌃⌘, press P two or more times, then release
      → Switches to the selected mode when you let go (like ⌘Tab)
  Esc, click outside the panel, or wait 10 seconds
      → Close without changing anything

To change the shortcut, choose "Change Shortcut (⌃⌘P)…" from the menu bar icon.


■ If a screen goes dark

  1. Press ⌃⌥⌘P (Control + Option + Command + P)
     → All displays are shown again (Extend)
  2. If that does not help, log out or restart

The first time you use a mode, a "Keep?" panel appears. If you don't click "Keep"
within 15 seconds, the previous display setup comes back automatically.


■ Uninstall

  1. Menu bar icon › turn off "Open at Login"
  2. Menu bar icon › "Quit Display Switcher"
  3. Move Display Switcher from the Applications folder to the Trash
